Location Description

Garden

Narrow garden in the house of Messius Ampliatus.

Keywords

  • courtyards (uncovered spaces)AAT:300004095
  • porticoesAAT:300004145

Garden Description

A. The portico which bordered the peristyle garden on the north and east side was accessed from the entrance hall. This site had been extensively damaged prior to excavation.

B. The east portico gave access to the narrow garden to the back of the house.
