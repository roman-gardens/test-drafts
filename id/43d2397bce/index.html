Theater

Authors: Wilhelmina Jashemski, Amina-Aïcha Malek
Contributor: Nicholas Gill
Published: 2021-04-21
Last modified: 2026-06-08

Dates

1st-2nd century CE

Garden Description

At the rear of the theater is a xystis known from the dedication inscription (CIL VIII 26606, 26608). The word xystis probably corresponds to the portico and the semi-circular garden space that develops behind the stage building.
