Province Description (Italia)

Italia was not considered a province until the reign of Diocletian. However, according to Pliny's Naturalis Historia, the peninsula was divided into eleven administrative regions by Augustus around 7 BCE:

  • Regio I: Latium et Campania
    • Includes major sites in Roma, Pompeii, and Herculaneum
  • Regio II: Apulia et Calabria
  • Regio III: Lucania et Bruttium
  • Regio IV: Samnium
  • Regio V: Picenum
  • Regio VI: Umbria et Ager Gallicus
  • Regio VII: Etruria
  • Regio VIII: Aemilia
  • Regio IX: Liguria
  • Regio X: Venetia et Histria
  • Regio XI: Transpadana

Place Description

Around 7 BCE, Samnium defined as Regio IV of Italia by Augustus.
