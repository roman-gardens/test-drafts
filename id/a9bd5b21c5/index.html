Altar of the Twelve Gods

Author: Maureen Carroll
Contributors: Jane Millar, Amartya Shri
Published: 2021-04-21
Last modified: 2026-06-08

Dates

late 1st century CE (mentioned)

Garden Description

A grove of olive and laurel trees surrounding the Altar of the Twelve Gods in the Athenian Agora is mentioned in the late 1st century CE by the Roman writer Statius in his epic poem Thebaid (12.481-496). Excavations conducted there revealed the remains of the planting pits of trees, although there appears to have been a very limited number, only three or four pits.
