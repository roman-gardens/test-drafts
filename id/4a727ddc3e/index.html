Province Description (Italia)

Italia was not considered a province until the reign of Diocletian. However, according to Pliny's Naturalis Historia, the peninsula was divided into eleven administrative regions by Augustus around 7 BCE:

  • Regio I: Latium et Campania
    • Includes major sites in Roma, Pompeii, and Herculaneum
  • Regio II: Apulia et Calabria
  • Regio III: Lucania et Bruttium
  • Regio IV: Samnium
  • Regio V: Picenum
  • Regio VI: Umbria et Ager Gallicus
  • Regio VII: Etruria
  • Regio VIII: Aemilia
  • Regio IX: Liguria
  • Regio X: Venetia et Histria
  • Regio XI: Transpadana

Place Description (Latium et Campania)

Around 7 BCE, Latium et Campania were defined as Regio I of Italia by Augustus. This region includes major archaeological sites in Roma, Pompeii, and Herculaneum.

Place Description (Pompeii)

Pompeii is an ancient city of Campania that was destroyed by the volcanic eruption of Mt. Vesuvius in CE 79. Pompeii was named as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.

Garden Description

According to Eschebach's plan, there was a portico at the rear of this house that enclosed this peristyle garden (a) on four sides. A small section of the foundation wall remains on the south end of the east side. The cubiculum (b) to the east of the atrium has a garden representation on the west half of the black predella on the south wall. It was poorly preserved but the left section shows a garden with a lattice fence and a pergola. Faint images remain of a similar pergola on the right side. The center of the painting, (which is 0.09 x 0.84 m.) has an open area with a crater perhaps serving as a fountain in a pergola. Two additional pergolas stand to either side.
